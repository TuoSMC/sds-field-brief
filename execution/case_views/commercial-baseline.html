This snapshot shows Forecast Master item-to-product matching and contact routing; it does not measure forecast accuracy or operational readiness. The export has no forecast quantity, requested date, booking / order actual, inventory, inbound ETA, capacity, pack-out, or shipping actuals. Buyer / DPM routes identify whom to contact—not authority to approve substitutions, final configurations, inventory commitments, or shipments. Storage routing is brand-level, not exact-SKU coverage. PGM must join and validate operating data before a decision.此快照只呈現 Forecast Master 品項到產品的對應，以及聯絡 routing；不衡量 forecast accuracy 或 operational readiness。此匯出未包含 forecast quantity、requested date、booking／order actual、inventory、inbound ETA、capacity、pack-out 或 shipping actuals。Buyer／DPM routing 只代表應聯絡對象，不代表替代料、最終配置、庫存承諾或出貨的核准權限。Storage routing 亦僅為品牌層 routing，不是 exact-SKU coverage。PGM 必須在決策前串接並驗證營運資料。
