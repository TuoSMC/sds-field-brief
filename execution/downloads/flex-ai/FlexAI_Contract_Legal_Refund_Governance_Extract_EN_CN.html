Decision-Rights & Legal Coordination Brief

A sanitized demonstration of how contract facts, Legal questions, commercial requests, and preservation needs were separated, routed, and controlled inside a cross-functional program. 這份匿名化文件展示：如何在跨部門 program 中，把 contract facts、Legal questions、commercial requests 與 preservation needs 分開、轉交並受控管理。

Not legal advice · not the original internal Legal referral · no source reproduction

The program organized the question. Authorized owners decided the answer.

Program leadership maintained the factual baseline, chronology, owner map, decision deadline, and distribution discipline. It did not decide breach, causation, liability, refund entitlement, or remedy. Program leadership 維持 factual baseline、chronology、owner map、decision deadline 與 distribution discipline；但不自行決定 breach、causation、liability、refund entitlement 或 remedy。

01 · Contract facts

Verify before interpreting

Use the executed-source baseline; separate operative text, missing documents, assumptions, and Legal implications.以 executed-source baseline 為準，拆開 operative text、缺少文件、assumptions 與 Legal implications。

02 · Legal questions

Route exact decisions

Frame the narrow question, relevant facts, source grade, open gaps, and requested decision—without writing counsel's conclusion.把問題、相關 facts、source grade、open gaps 與 requested decision 寫清楚，不代替 counsel 下結論。

03 · Commercial asks

Document without conceding

Record refund, complimentary PoC, and compensation requests while preserving authorization boundaries.記錄 refund、complimentary PoC 與 compensation requests，同時保留授權邊界。

04 · Preservation

Protect the record

Identify relevant source categories, custodians, handling rules, and owner instructions before records disappear or change.在資料消失或改變前，確認 source categories、custodians、handling rules 與 owner instructions。

Refund control

Document the request. Protect the decision.

Capture

Record what was requested, when, through which source, and which facts remain unverified.記錄要求內容、時間、source，以及仍未確認的 facts。

Route

Assign Finance, Legal, and management ownership; define the next decision and approval path.指定 Finance、Legal 與 management owner，明確下一個 decision 與 approval path。

Control

Keep acknowledgment separate from entitlement; do not promise credit, refund, replacement, or remedy without authority.把 acknowledgment 與 entitlement 分開；未獲授權不得承諾 credit、refund、replacement 或 remedy。

Decision rights

What Program leadership can own—and what it must route.

Program / Sales can own

  • One factual baseline and dated chronology
  • Source grade, open gaps, and correction log
  • Named owner, next decision, and follow-up cadence
  • Approved factual communication and handoff
  • Secure document handling and audience control

Authorized owners retain

  • Contract interpretation and legal effect
  • Causation, breach, liability, and remedy
  • Refund entitlement and commercial approval
  • Final technical disposition and formal closure
  • Preservation or litigation-hold scope
