Executive conclusion

What I did—and what I deliberately did not decide.

Strong program leadership creates control without impersonating specialist authority.強力的 program leadership 是建立控制力，而不是冒充其他專業部門的決策權。

Action

Internal records place me as account coordinator / SPOC and record RMA coordination plus Legal routing. I maintained one factual baseline and a dated owner cadence.Internal records 將我記錄為 account coordinator／SPOC，並記錄 RMA coordination 與 Legal routing；我維持一套事實基準及有日期的 owner cadence。

Decision discipline

I separated recorded operational recovery from formal technical closure and kept causation, liability, refund entitlement, and remedy with authorized owners.我把已記錄的 operational recovery 與正式 technical closure 分開，並將 causation、liability、refund entitlement 與 remedy 留給授權 owner。

Result

Direct artifact: final diagnostic PASS after 32h12m. Internal record: reinstallation one day ahead and commercial requests routed.
